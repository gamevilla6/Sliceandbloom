---
name: Reward economy boundaries
description: Why the reward wheel uses free, non-transferable virtual credits and requires a policy review before paid stakes.
---

Keep the Reward Hub's wager currency free-only and its prizes non-transferable.
Do not extend the wheel to purchased stakes, cash-out or tradable prizes without
a fresh product and policy review.

**Why:** The hub was designed as an optional rewarded-ad feature, not real-value
gambling. Google permits disclosed random in-app rewards but prohibits direct
monetary rewards; adding paid stakes changes this feature's risk profile.

**How to apply:** Review this boundary before adding purchases, currency trading
or new wheel payouts. Random ad rewards must disclose their possible prizes and
odds before opt-in. Native ad cancellation must never block normal gameplay.
