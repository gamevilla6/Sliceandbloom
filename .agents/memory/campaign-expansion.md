---
name: Campaign expansion direction
description: How the advanced-mechanics brief should fit the existing game
---

Introduce the advanced mechanics as new levels after the existing 26, rather than replacing the opening campaign.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose to append advanced levels and keep the current Arcade Pop UI when applying the uploaded game brief.

**How to apply:** Preserve the original campaign and earned progress when extending it. Do not treat the brief's opening-level examples or pastel styling as permission to replace the established game.

Hardcore is a separate, optional 100-level mode. Levels 46–100 extend the original Hardcore set; preserve the first 45 levels and their records.

**Why:** The user chose a separate Hardcore mode, then explicitly expanded its level count while preserving the existing campaign and other modes.

**How to apply:** Keep mode progression and ratings independent. Append new levels without changing the original 45 Hardcore levels or their earned stars.

The Genesis Gauntlet is a third, completely separate arcade mode with 100 unique Masterclass Challenges, not another name for Campaign or Hardcore. Preserve its original 45 when extending it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a separate Genesis challenge set and then 55 new stages with reflected lasers, pulley chains and two-droplet puzzles. Its scores, best times and stars must remain independent.

**How to apply:** Preserve all three modes and keep their records independent. Genesis modifiers must affect the actual physics and budgets, not just visual presentation. The local leaderboard shows personal device records, not invented global rankings.

Infinity Cascade extends the main Campaign with Levels 46–100; it is not another independent progression mode.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested Infinity Cascade as a campaign expansion covering Levels 46–100, alongside the separate Genesis Gauntlet.

**How to apply:** Keep the opening 45 campaign levels and their saves intact. Campaign, Genesis and Hardcore each have 100 independent stages. Category navigation distinguishes each mode and its ranges.

The Void Protocol is a fourth, dedicated 110-level mode, not a replacement for the existing Hardcore mode.

**Why:** The user requested a separate 55-level Void mode with its own leaderboards, deaths and completion badges, then requested 55 more levels.

**How to apply:** Append Void Levels 56–110 without changing the first 55 or their earned records. Preserve Campaign, Hardcore and Genesis saves. Keep Void records independent and identify its leaderboard as personal device records. Manual retries are not deaths; a lost attempt counts exactly once.
