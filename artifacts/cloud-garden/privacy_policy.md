# Slice & Bloom Privacy Policy

**Developer:** Game Villa  
**Support:** [gameover0134@gmail.com](mailto:gameover0134@gmail.com)  
**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

This policy explains what Slice & Bloom stores on your device and how
advertising works in the game.

## Game data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, records, settings, and in-game reward items are
saved locally on your device or browser so they are available when you play
again. The game does not require an account. This information is not uploaded
to a Game Villa game server.

## Advertising and consent

Where ads are available, the game uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent
tools. Ads may include banners, interstitials after eligible wins, and optional
rewarded videos. Rewarded items are granted by the game only after the ad
reports a completed reward.

Ad requests are configured as non-personalized. Google may still process
information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, consent
choices, and ad or diagnostic events to provide, secure, and measure its
services. Google's handling of that information is described in its own
[Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Analytics and sharing

The current game code does not include a separate analytics service or send
gameplay progress to a Game Villa server. Google receives the information
needed for its advertising and consent services when those services are used.
If you choose to email support, your email app handles the message.

## Your choices

You can play without choosing to watch a rewarded video. You can remove
locally saved game data by clearing the app's storage in your device or browser
settings; this also removes local progress and rewards. Any consent choices
offered by Google's consent tools apply to ad requests on that device.
