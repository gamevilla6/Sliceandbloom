Welcome to Slice & Bloom, a colorful 2D physics puzzle game by Game Villa! Draw paths, slice ropes and guide a water droplet toward a dry flower. Solve creative puzzles where gravity, timing and a carefully placed stroke can make the garden bloom.

INTERNET CONNECTION REQUIRED
An active internet connection is required to start and continue playing. If your connection drops, gameplay pauses until connectivity is restored. Rewarded hint videos also require connectivity and depend on ad availability.

KEY FEATURES

465 PHYSICS PUZZLES
Explore 100 Campaign beds, 100 Hardcore levels, 100 Genesis Gauntlet challenges, 110 Void Protocol levels and 55 Singularity Paradox puzzles.

DRAW, SLICE AND BLOOM
Draw ramps and cut ropes with one-touch controls. Work with moving obstacles, portals, gravity changes and dangerous hazards to help water reach each flower.

OPTIONAL REWARDED HINTS
Choose to watch an available rewarded video to reveal a helpful cutting or drawing guide. Rewards are credited only after the ad reports an earned reward and closes.

LOCAL PROGRESS AND PERSONAL RECORDS
Your level unlocks, stars, personal records and reward inventory are saved on your device. No player account is required. There are no cloud saves, cross-device progress sync or online leaderboards in this version.

RESPONSIVE PLAY
Browse scrollable level chapters and play on supported Android phones and tablets. Retry challenging stages and improve your own results.
